defined by `verification.md`; those documents and the requirements/specification
remain authoritative. A passing test demonstrates only the named observations
and level actually exercised. It does not promote a mock/scripted integration to
realistic evidence or satisfy unexecuted variants. P5 is **not complete**, and
this is not deployment, product acceptance, or authorization to contact external
services.

## Gaps and boundaries

- The evidence above is an offline integration subset, not a scenario-complete
  acceptance matrix. The realistic levels requiring Trello, GitHub, OpenCode or
  enabled email remain **blocked/unexecuted**: separate authorization, isolated
  resources, credentials and operator permission were not supplied. No live
  mutation or credential access was attempted.
- A2 semantic support/rubric decisions and the outstanding A5 progress/manual
  move policy are not approved by this evidence map. No semantic rubric is
  inferred from tests. Exact clarification and long-progress/move boundary
  judgments remain blocked on decisions.
- The first P3 Podman run failed solely because its container omitted `TMPDIR`;
  retained history includes the failure. The test command now sets a private
  container `/tmp` and the same bounded build/run command passed. No source
  upgrade artifacts or historical probe artifacts were deleted or rewritten.
- These gaps do not authorize speculative later-phase implementation. Further
  autonomous evidence work is possible for local deterministic gaps; realistic
  external scenarios and unresolved A2/A5 policy decisions cannot proceed
  without authorization/resources or decisions.
